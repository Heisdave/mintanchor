03 · Loop simulator

Rehearse the loop first

Looping is borrowing MUSD, swapping it to BTC and depositing again, round after round. It multiplies gains and liquidation risk together. Rehearse it here and see the leverage, the final ratio and the price that would liquidate you, before any real loop.

Honest label: the simulator treats MUSD at exactly 1 dollar and swaps at the live BTC price with no slippage and no fees. Interest is excluded; at Mezo's current 1% APR it is small next to price risk inside a stress horizon. Real loops will differ.

04 · On-chain snapshot

Save it where it counts

A stress test you cannot find again is a rumour. Save the current stress result to the RiskRegistry contract on Mezo testnet: collateral ratio, liquidation price, risk band and your label, stored on chain and readable by anyone.

Saves are signed by the desk demo wallet on testnet and labelled as such. A snapshot is a public notebook entry, not a financial record.

Mezo testnet only · No real funds

MintAnchor runs entirely on Mezo testnet (chain 31611). Positions are read live from Mezo Borrow contracts. Stress tests and loop simulations are illustrations built on the live price and Mezo's published borrow terms (110% minimum collateral ratio, 150% critical ratio, 1% fixed APR at time of writing). They are not financial advice and they are not a promise about any real position.
